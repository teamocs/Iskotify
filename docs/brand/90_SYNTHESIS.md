# Iskotify — Brandbook (synthesis)

Status: complete (2026-10-12) · single participant (founder) · sources: modules 10–70 in `modules/`

Use this to brief designers, writers and collaborators. Kapferer prism + Aaker system, reduced to what is actionable.

## 1. Purpose
No Filipino student should miss college because nobody told them it was free — or because they couldn't pay for review.
Rooted in the founder's family struggle to afford college; the gap was **knowing** which scholarships existed and when they closed.

## 2. Positioning
For Filipino senior high students aiming for college who can't afford to miss a scholarship, Iskotify is the kuya in your pocket: free review for UPCAT, ACET, DCAT and more, plus reminders of what's open, what you qualify for and when it closes — with the official source. Unlike scholarship Facebook pages and overstretched teachers, it tells you **before** the deadline, not after.
- Category in students' words: *"parang kuya na nagpapaalala ng deadlines."*
- Core value (what she'd keep): knowing what's open and when it closes.

## 3. Narrative
- **Door (pitch):** "Free review for students who can't afford review centers."
- **Surprise (product):** it tracks the scholarships she qualifies for and reminds her in time.
- **Ending:** *"I didn't miss my scholarship this time."*
- Trueline: Free review for students who can't afford review centers — and a kuya who makes sure you don't miss what you qualify for.

## 4. Audience
**Primary: the Iskolar Aspirant** — public-school Grade 11–12 student; budget Android shared with siblings; prepaid load; studies at night after chores. Trusts **proof**: the official source link and exact date.
Secondary (welcome, not designed-for): Anxious Achiever, Barkada Reviewer, parents.

## 5. Personality
Archetype: **Caregiver** (protective older sibling) + **Sage** (knows what's open).
Traits: calm · steady · warm · knowing · plain-spoken · protective.
Never: scares her, says she's "behind", shows verdicts or fake percentiles, hypes.

## 6. Voice & tone
- **English everywhere.** Only exception: the tagline **"Para sa mga Iskolar ng Bayan."**
- Warm like a kuya, exact like a registrar: **encourage, then cite.**
- Every deadline: exact date + official source + one action.
  > "Heads up: DOST-SEI closes Oct 20, 8 days from now. You've got this. Let's finish your requirements today. Source: sei.dost.gov.ph"
- Don't: "behind", "only … left!", stacked exclamation marks, emoji, Taglish in UI.
- Tone table by moment: see `modules/50_voice-tone.md`.

## 7. Identity
- **Logo: the current app logo stays** (pixel-art carabao with graduation cap on #831626). A redraw was explored and rejected by the owner (2026-10-12) — do not propose a new mark unless asked.
- Palette: Refined Maroon tokens unchanged (DESIGN.md). Light-only.
- Type: system font (SF Pro / Roboto) per the 2026-10 iOS-language redesign.
- UI language: iOS-inspired (see design canvas https://claude.ai/artifact/KgbdzhzMm4fSchSeyUMB28 and `docs/design/ios-redesign-plan-2026-10.md`).

## 8. Brand architecture
- **Iskotify is a product brand**; the founder stays behind the scenes (third-person origin story, no founder photo).
- **Review Masters Bicol** is credited in text as content partner (footer, About, store description) — not a co-brand on icon, splash or hero.
