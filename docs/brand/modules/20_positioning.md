# 20 — Positioning

Status: done (2026-10-12)

## Raw

- Q: If a student could only keep one half of Iskotify — the prep, or knowing what's open and when it closes — which should it be?
  A: "Knowing what's open and when it closes"
- Q: Where do students actually go today to learn which scholarships are open and when they close?
  A: "Facebook pages and their teachers mostly"
- Q: When a student relies on Facebook pages or their teacher, what goes wrong most often?
  A: "Deadlines already passed by the time they saw it"
- Q: When a student tells a friend about Iskotify in one line, what do you want them to say it is?
  A: "Parang kuya na nagpapaalala ng deadlines" (like an older brother who reminds you of deadlines)

## Synthesis

**Competitive alternatives (Dunford):** scholarship Facebook pages and teachers/guidance counselors. Both free and trusted; both *pull* — the student only learns what crosses their feed or what the teacher happens to know.

**Failure of the alternatives:** timing. The student sees the opportunity after it has closed.

**Unique attribute → value:** Iskotify knows the student's exams, target schools and eligibility, and *pushes* the right opening and deadline to them before it closes; then helps them prepare for the exam behind it. Value: you don't miss what you qualified for.

**Category frame:** not "reviewer app", not "scholarship database". It is a *kuya* — a personal guide that reminds you in time. Category language for marketing: "your scholarship and exam kuya" / "deadline kuya". Prep is the supporting capability, not the headline.

**Moore positioning statement (draft):**
For Filipino senior high students aiming for college who can't afford to miss a scholarship, Iskotify is the kuya in your pocket that tells you what's open, what you qualify for and when it closes, then gets you ready for the exam. Unlike scholarship Facebook pages and overstretched teachers, it reminds you before the deadline, not after.

**Three candidate one-liners:**
1. "Parang kuya na nagpapaalala ng deadlines." (founder's own words — strongest)
2. "Know what's open. Make it in time."
3. "Hindi ka na mahuhuli." (You won't be late again.)

**Product/brand implications:**
- Today screen should lead with deadlines + what you qualify for; prep is the "one next step" toward a specific door.
- The App Store / landing hero should sell timing ("before it closes"), not question volume.
- The redesign canvas currently leads Today with a prep drill; flag for P4 Today PR: make "Coming up" deadlines co-equal or first. (Raised for owner confirmation; not changed yet.)

**Logo implications:** the mark should carry *kuya* (protective older-sibling guide) + *timeliness* (a nudge, a bell, a calendar tick) more than *exam*. Strong support for evolving the carabao (Kuya Baw) as the carrier, with the grad cap optional.

**Open questions:** whether parents are a messaging target (module 30); whether "kuya" is gendered in a way that matters to the audience (module 40).
