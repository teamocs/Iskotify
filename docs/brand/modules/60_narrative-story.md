# 60 — Narrative / Story

Status: done (2026-10-12)

## Raw

- Q: Tell the story in one breath to a public high school guidance counselor in Bicol.
  A: "Free review for students who can't afford review centers"
- Q: When pitching, which comes first — free review (deadlines as the surprise inside) or deadlines (review as support)?
  A: "Free review first, deadlines as the surprise"
- Q: A year from now, what's the one sentence you'd love to hear her say about Iskotify?
  A: "I didn't miss my scholarship this time"

## Synthesis

**Resolved tension (positioning vs pitch):** free review is the *door* (acquisition: what counselors share and students download); deadlines are the *surprise* (retention and real outcome: why she keeps it). Pitch leads with free review; the product experience lands on "it told me in time". Both modules stand: positioning = what Iskotify *is* at its core; narrative = how people *find* it.

**Trueline (Neumeier):** "Free review for students who can't afford review centers — and a kuya who makes sure you don't miss what you qualify for."

**Story arc:**
1. *The world:* scholarships exist so money won't decide who goes to college.
2. *The problem:* students who can't pay for review centers prepare alone and hear about scholarships from scattered Facebook posts, often after the deadline.
3. *The origin:* the founder watched their own family struggle to afford college.
4. *The answer:* Iskotify — free review on the phone she already has, plus a kuya that reminds her what's open, what she qualifies for and when it closes, with the official source.
5. *The ending:* "I didn't miss my scholarship this time."

**Three candidate story lines:**
1. Counselor pitch (15 s): "Iskotify is free review for students who can't afford review centers. It also tracks the scholarships they qualify for and reminds them before each deadline, with the official source."
2. Student pitch: "Free UPCAT, ACET and DCAT review. And it reminds you before scholarship deadlines close."
3. Brand closer: "This time, she didn't miss it."

**Implications:**
- Store listing: lead "Free review…", second line reveals deadlines. (Landing page excluded: its approved content stays as is.)
- Onboarding: start with exam picker (the door), then reveal deadlines tracked for her ("the surprise") as an early wow moment.
- Testimonials/campaigns: collect "I didn't miss it" stories.
