# 30 — Audience & Niche

Status: done (2026-10-12)

## Raw

- Q: Of the four personas (Iskolar Aspirant, Anxious Achiever, Sacrificing Parent, Barkada Reviewer), which one is the brand built for first?
  A: "Iskolar Aspirant"
- Q: Picture one real Iskolar Aspirant: what phone, how does she get online, where does she study?
  A: "Budget Android shared with siblings, prepaid load, studies at night"
- Q: What would make her trust an app enough to stop double-checking Facebook?
  A: "Seeing the official source link and deadline"

## Synthesis

**Primary audience (ICP):** the Iskolar Aspirant — a Grade 11–12 public-school student for whom a scholarship decides whether college happens. Everyone else (Anxious Achiever, Barkada Reviewer, parents) is welcome, but brand decisions are judged by her reaction first.

**Context of use:**
- Budget Android, shared with siblings → small, low-DPI screens; the app icon must read at ~48 px with no fine detail; notification text must not expose private data on a shared lock screen; account switching / sign-out should be easy.
- Prepaid load → data-light everything (system font, small assets, offline-first already in the product).
- Studies at night, after chores → sessions are short and tired; reminders and the One Next Step must be low-effort. (Tension: product is light-only by owner decision 2026-09-27 — logged as open question, not a change.)

**Trust driver:** proof, not persuasion. Every deadline shows its official source link and the exact date. Brand voice should cite, not hype. This is the reason to choose Iskotify over Facebook reposts.

**Candidate formulations:**
1. "Built for the student who can't afford to miss it."
2. "Official dates. In time. On the phone you already have."
3. "Para sa Iskolar ng Bayan — kahit shared ang phone, kahit gabi ka lang makapag-aral."

**Implications:**
- Logo: bold, simple silhouette; ≤3 shapes; legible in one colour; works on the Android adaptive mask at 48 px.
- Product (flag for owner): show "Source: [official link]" and the exact deadline on every listing card and reminder (likely already partially present via admin listings; verify in P4 Explore PR).

**Open questions:** parents as a secondary messaging audience (deferred; not the brand's first audience).
