# 10 — Purpose / Why

Status: done (2026-10-12)

## Raw

- Q: What moment, or which student, made you decide Iskotify had to exist?
  A: "Seeing students who couldn't afford review centers miss scholarships"
- Q: When one of those students missed the scholarship, what did they lose?
  A: "A chance at college without burdening their family"
- Q: Why does "without burdening their family" matter so much to you?
  A: "I saw my own family struggle to afford college"
- Q: Looking back, what would have made the biggest difference for your family then?
  A: "Knowing which scholarships existed and their deadlines"

## Synthesis

**The ladder:** students miss scholarships → they lose college without the family burden → the founder lived that struggle → what was missing most was *knowing* (which scholarships exist, and when they close).

**Core value: fair access to opportunity.** Money and insider knowledge should not decide who gets a scholarship. The founder's own sense of the injustice is mostly about information, not ability: the opportunities existed, but nobody told them in time.

**Implication for the brand:** Iskotify's deepest job is to be the *older sibling who knows* — the one who tells you what exists, what's due, and what to do next. Exam prep is the means; "nobody misses out because they didn't know" is the end. This fits the existing Kuya Baw mascot ("kuya" = older brother) and the approved tagline "Para sa mga Iskolar ng Bayan".

**Three candidate purpose statements:**
1. "No Filipino student should miss college because nobody told them it was free."
2. "We make sure every student knows every door that's open to them — and gets through it on time."
3. "Para walang maiwan: the opportunities are out there; we make sure you see them before they close."

**Open questions (for later modules):**
- Positioning: is Iskotify primarily a scholarship/deadline navigator that also preps, or a prep app that also tracks scholarships? The founder's answer leans to the former; the product today leads with prep.
- Audience: does "family" make parents a secondary audience for the brand voice (the Sacrificing Parent persona), even though they are not account holders?

**Contradictions:** none yet (single participant).

**Logo implications (provisional):** the mark should signal guidance and reaching something on time (an older-sibling guide, a path, an open door) more than "test prep". The carabao (patience, hard work, very Filipino) is a candidate carrier for that meaning — to be tested in Personality (module 40).
