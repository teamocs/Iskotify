# 70 — Founder Brand vs Product Brand

Status: done (2026-10-12)

## Raw

- Q: Should your own story appear publicly as the face behind Iskotify, or should Iskotify stand on its own with Review Masters Bicol credited as content partner?
  A: "Iskotify stands on its own, credit Review Masters Bicol"

## Synthesis

**Decision:** Iskotify is a product brand, not a founder brand. The founder stays behind the scenes; the origin story may be told in the third person ("Iskotify started with a family that couldn't afford college…") without naming or picturing the founder.

**Partner credit:** Review Masters Bicol is credited as the content partner — a trust signal for question quality, not a co-brand.
- Placement: footer and About ("Questions reviewed with Review Masters Bicol"), store listing description, and the question-review context in the admin console.
- Not placed: app icon, splash, tab bar, landing hero.
- Treatment: plain text credit (no RMB logo lockup with the Iskotify logo unless the owner later approves one).

**Implications:**
- About page: brand story in third person; no founder photo.
- Social posts speak as Iskotify ("we"), never as an individual.
- Landing footer already reads "with Review Masters Bicol" — keep.

**Open questions:** none.
