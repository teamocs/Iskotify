# 40 — Personality & Archetype

Status: done (2026-10-12)

## Raw

- Q: If Iskotify walked into a room full of nervous Grade 12 students, how would it walk in — what does it do first, what would it never do?
  A: "Calm and confident, reminds them what's due first"
- Q: What would this kuya never do in that room?
  A: "Scare them by saying they're behind"
- Q: Is the carabao (Kuya Baw) still the right carrier, or should the mark go abstract?
  A: "Keep the carabao, but make it modern and minimal"

## Synthesis

**Archetype (Mark & Pearson):** Caregiver (primary) — the protective older sibling; Sage (secondary) — it knows what's open and when. Explicitly NOT Hero (no "crush the exam"), NOT Jester, NOT Ruler.

**Aaker dimensions:** Sincerity (high: honest, down-to-earth, wholesome) + Competence (high: reliable, organised) > Excitement (low-medium) > Sophistication (low) > Ruggedness (low).

**Behaviour rules:**
- Leads with what's due, calmly.
- Never scares: no "you're behind", no red verdicts, no fake percentiles, no countdown panic. Gaps are framed as a doable next step ("2 sessions to catch up this week").
- Confident through facts (official source + date), not volume.

**Brand traits (pick list for designers):** calm · steady · protective · knowing · warm · plain-spoken.

**Mascot/mark decision (final, 2026-10-12):** a modern minimal redraw was explored and **rejected by the owner** ("it was awful"). The current app logo — pixel-art carabao with graduation cap on #831626 — stays as the logo. Do not propose a new mark unless the owner asks.

**Candidate formulations:**
1. "The calm kuya who always knows what's due."
2. "Steady like a carabao. Always on time."
3. "Hindi ka niya tatakutin. Paaalalahanan ka lang niya." (He won't scare you. He'll just remind you.)
