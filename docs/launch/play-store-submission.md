## Store listing

**App name (30 max):** Iskotify: UPCAT and CET Review

**Short description (80 max):** Practice for UPCAT and other entrance exams. Find scholarships and deadlines.

**Full description:**

Iskotify helps Filipino senior high school students get ready for college.

PRACTICE
- Timed mock exams for UPCAT, ACET, USTET, DCAT and other entrance tests
- A short diagnostic that shows where to start
- 30-minute Study Sprints and subject drills
- Over 4,000 practice questions with explanations
- Flashcards that come back when you are about to forget them

DECIDE
- Scholarships, entrance exams, schools and courses in one place
- Over 170 scholarship listings with requirements
- More than 700 schools, with board exam results for many courses
- Match scholarships to your profile, only if you choose to share those details

KEEP TRACK
- Exam dates and deadlines, with a link to the official source where we have one
- A daily study plan that counts down to your exam
- Progress by subject, accuracy and pace
- Notes and reminders

Practice works offline after the first download. Sign in to back up your progress.

Iskotify is an independent study app by Online Creative Solutions. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the University of the Philippines or any school, testing center or scholarship provider. The Estimated Admission Score is based on historical cutoffs and is not an admission decision. Always check dates and requirements on the official website.
